Website Product QR Portal

Generate Product QR Codes & Check Live Stock Availability — Right on the Product Page

Overview

Turn every product into a smart, scannable gateway. Generate a QR code directly from the product form, print it on any box or shelf label, and let customers scan it to instantly view product details and check real-time stock availability — no login, no app, no manual work.

Key Features

⚡ Instant QR Generation

One click from the product form. No external QR tools, no manual work.

📱 Zero-Login Scan Access

Anyone scans, anyone views. No account, no app download required.

✅ Live Stock Availability Check

Customers enter a quantity and instantly see if it's in stock — before checkout.

🌐 Auto Product Landing Page

Public page with name, price, category, barcode, reference, and variants.

🛒 Native eCommerce Integration

Built directly into the website_sale product page. No redirects, no clunky setup.

🔒 Secure Role-Based Access

Full access for staff, restricted and safe access for public visitors.

How It Works

1
Open Product Form

Go to any product in Odoo.

2
Generate QR Code

Click one button to create it.

3
Print & Attach

Stick it on the box, shelf, or label.

4
Customer Scans

Instant product info & stock check.
