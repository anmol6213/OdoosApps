Key Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

QR Generation Public Access Stock Availability Integration & Security
Instant QR Generation

Generate a scannable QR code directly from the product form with a single click. No external QR tools, no manual image handling, no third-party services required.

  • One-click generation from the product form
  • Ready to print on boxes, shelves, or labels
  • Works out of the box - zero configuration
Zero-Login Public Access

Every QR code opens an auto-generated public landing page. Customers see product details instantly - no account, no app download, no friction.

  • Auto product landing page: name, price, category
  • Displays barcode, reference, and variants
  • Anyone scans, anyone views - instantly
Live Stock Availability Check

Customers can enter a desired quantity on the scanned page and instantly see whether it's in stock, before they ever reach checkout.

  • Real-time inventory check on scan
  • Quantity-based availability lookup
  • Reduces pre-checkout stock surprises
Native Integration & Secure Access

Built directly into the website_sale product page with no redirects or clunky setup, backed by role-based access control.

  • Native eCommerce (website_sale) integration
  • Full access for staff, restricted access for public
  • Compatible with Odoo Community and Enterprise
